The Problem We Resolve

Madam Hexley runs the busiest potion shop in the village. Every Halloween she runs out of Bat Wings, Mandrake Root, and Ghost Pepper at the worst moment, and angry customers turn into frogs!

The Spooky Pantry provides a smart, automated inventory command center that resolves every piece of her dilemma:

  • Real-Time Tracking: Every potion purchase decrements MongoDB atomically.
  • Prophecy (Forecasting): Projects exact runout dates with day-of-week coven gathering weighting.
  • Restock Spell: Dynamic ROP formulas calculate optimal reorders without overspending.
  • Strange Happenings: 100% precision Z-score outlier detection flags consumption spikes.
  • Midnight Curse: Instantly responds when demand for festival ingredients triples (3.0×).
